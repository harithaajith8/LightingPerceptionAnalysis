How the experiment worked

The task

Landolt C sheets with 64 rings each. Participants marked the opening of every C. Four sheet versions, A and B with larger rings, C and D smaller.

The light

Two booths with 3200 K LED engines at 20, 80, 320 and 800 lux. Uniformity above 0.80, lighting order randomised, experimenters blind to the setting.

The ratings

After each trial: lighting satisfaction, task difficulty, effort and self-rated performance, from 1 (good) to 7 (poor).

For lighting design: satisfaction and visual performance are different outcomes. Measure both before treating a brighter room as a better one.

Limits: one task (Landolt C), four light levels, student participants, so differences between levels are modest and individual variation is large. Next steps: colour temperature, task complexity, larger samples. DESC9201 Indoor Environmental Quality, Faculty of Architecture, Design and Planning, University of Sydney.
